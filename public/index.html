FAQ

Questions people usually ask.

How do I start?

Open the Website Planner and answer a few simple questions about your business and what you need. I'll review it and get back to you, usually within 24 hours. You can also just message me.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website is designed mobile-first and tested on small phones, tablets and desktops before launch.

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at ₹4,999 and business websites at ₹9,999. The final price depends on pages and features, and you get a clear quote before any work begins. See pricing.

Is SEO included?

Every site includes an SEO foundation: page titles, descriptions, proper headings, fast loading and a sitemap. Ongoing SEO work like content writing and link building is a separate service.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Two rounds of revisions are included at the design stage and again before launch, so the result feels right to you.
