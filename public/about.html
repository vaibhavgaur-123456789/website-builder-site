About

Hi, I'm Vaibhav Gaur 👋. I build websites that work as hard as you do.

I run Vaibhav Web Studio from Ayodhya and work with clients across India. My focus is simple: websites that load fast, look professional on a phone, and make it easy for customers to get in touch.

A lot of small businesses either don't have a website or have one that's slow, outdated or impossible to update. Good websites shouldn't only be for big companies. That's why I started building them.

I'm also a technical electrician. Electrical work taught me to do things properly: plan before building, follow the spec and test everything before handing it over. I work the same way on websites.

What I bring

More than just code.

💻 Web design & development

Planning, design and hand-written code for fast, responsive websites, from single landing pages to online stores.

🖼 Thumbnails & visual design

I design YouTube thumbnails, which has taught me what grabs attention in a split second. I bring that to every hero section and call to action.

🎬 Content creation

As a content creator I know how to write and structure things so people actually read them. That helps when shaping the words on your website.

🤖 AI-assisted workflow

I use modern AI tools to research, draft and build faster, which keeps your costs down. Every design and every line of code is still reviewed by me.

My approach

How I work with clients

  • Plain language. I explain everything without jargon, so you can make decisions with confidence.
  • Your goals first. Every page is planned around what you want visitors to do: call, book, buy or enquire.
  • Honest advice. If you don't need a feature, I'll tell you. If a cheaper option works better, I'll suggest it.
  • Direct contact. You talk to the person building your website, on WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • No lock-in. Once it's paid for, the website and its content are yours.
