Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers, in plain language. If your question isn't here, just ask.

Getting started

What type of websites do you build?

Business and service websites, landing pages, portfolios, personal brand sites, blogs and online stores. I also take on custom projects like booking systems and calculators. See services for details.

How do I start?

The easiest way is the Website Planner. It asks simple questions about your business and what you need, and takes about 5 minutes. You can also message me on WhatsApp or use the contact form.

I don't know exactly what I need. Is that okay?

Completely fine, most people don't. Answer what you can in the planner and choose “Not sure” for the rest. I'll recommend what makes sense for your business.

Can you build a custom website?

Yes. Every website is designed for your business, not taken from a ready-made theme. For custom features, I'll tell you honestly what's involved and what it will cost before we start.

Design, speed & SEO

Is the website mobile responsive?

Yes. I design for phones first, because that's where most of your visitors will be, then adapt the layout for tablets and computers. Every site is tested at multiple screen sizes before launch.

Is SEO included?

Every website includes an SEO foundation: unique page titles and descriptions, proper heading structure, image alt text, fast loading, a sitemap and help submitting your site to Google Search Console. Ongoing SEO (regular content, local listings, backlinks) is a separate service. No one can honestly guarantee a #1 ranking.

Can you optimise page speed?

Yes. I compress and resize images, keep code lean, avoid heavy plugins and load things only when they're needed. If you already have a slow website, I can review it and tell you what's slowing it down.

Can you integrate forms?

Yes: contact forms, quote requests, booking requests and more. Enquiries can go straight to your email, and I add quiet spam protection so you get real messages without making visitors solve puzzles.

Process, pricing & support

How does the project process work?

1) You share your requirements. 2) We agree scope, price and timeline in writing. 3) I design the structure and look, and you give feedback. 4) I build and optimise the site. 5) We launch, and I support you for 30 days. See the full process.

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at ₹4,999, portfolios at ₹5,999, business websites at ₹9,999 and online stores at ₹24,999. Your domain, paid tools and payment-gateway fees are separate. You get a fixed quote before work begins. See pricing.

How long does it take?

Landing pages usually take about a week, business websites 1–3 weeks, and online stores 3–4 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly content (text, photos, logo) is ready.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Two rounds of revisions are included, and small fixes are free for 30 days after launch. Bigger changes or new features later are quoted separately, or covered by a monthly care plan.

Do I need my own domain and hosting?

You'll need a domain (your web address). I can help you buy one in your own name. For most websites I use fast, reliable hosting that's often free for small sites. I'll explain the options before we start.

How do payments work?

A 50% advance to start, and the remaining 50% before the website goes live. Payment is by UPI or bank transfer.
