How it works

A simple process with no surprises.

You'll know what's happening at every stage, and nothing goes live until you're happy with it.

  1. 💡 Tell me your idea

    Fill in the Website Planner, or just message me on WhatsApp. No technical knowledge needed.

    5 minutes

  2. 🤝 We agree the plan

    I review your answers, ask any questions, and send a written scope with price and timeline.

    1–2 days

  3. ✏ Structure & design

    I map out the pages and design the homepage first. You give feedback before development starts.

    3–7 days
